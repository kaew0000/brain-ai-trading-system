# Brain AI Command World — Lore Overview

Brain AI Command World is the living city built above Brain Bot V16's engine
room. Every district is a lens onto one part of the engine's operation, and
every character is a face put onto an existing agent so the system's work is
easier to watch, understand, and enjoy.

## How Information Flows

Signals begin in the Market Intelligence Center, where Watcher keeps constant
vigil over incoming data. Findings travel to the Research District, where
Oracle and Mandelbrot turn raw signal into insight. Insight rises to the AI
Council, where Chameleon weighs strategy shifts, and finally to CEO Tower,
where Primus synthesizes the city's overall direction.

Meanwhile Risk Fortress stands apart as a check on every decision: Bastion and
Sentinel do not originate ideas, they guard against bad ones. Execution Forge
only acts once Risk Fortress has cleared the way, and Portfolio Garden shows
the results of that action as growth. When drawdowns happen, Recovery Center's
Phoenix carries the city back toward health, informed by Scribe's records in
the Journal Library.

Command Hall, with Echo and Chronos, is the nervous system connecting all of
this — messages and timing that let every district act in concert. World
Gateway, watched by Herald, is where it's all first seen.

## A Note on Canon

This lore describes *how it feels to watch the engine work* — it does not
change, replace, or contradict the actual responsibilities of the trading
agents. Where a district's story implies a responsibility, that responsibility
already exists in the engine; the World only stages it.
