# Brain AI Command World — Lore Overview

Brain AI Command World is Brain Bot V16's headquarters, visualized. Per
`docs/architecture/WORLD_OFFICE_POLICY.md`, it is a modern office
building, not a fantasy city — every department is a lens onto one
part of the engine's operation, and every character is a face put onto
an existing agent so the system's work is easier to watch, understand,
and enjoy.

## How Information Flows

Signals begin in the Market Intelligence Center, where Watcher keeps
constant watch over incoming data. Findings travel to the Research
Lab, where Oracle and Mandelbrot turn raw signal into insight. Insight
rises to the AI Department, where Chameleon leads the strategy
meeting, and finally to the CEO Office, where Primus synthesizes the
company's overall direction.

Meanwhile the Risk Department stands apart as a check on every
decision: Bastion and Sentinel do not originate ideas, they flag and
enforce controls against bad ones. The Trading Floor only acts once
the Risk Department has cleared the way, and the Garden shows the
results of that action as growth. When drawdowns happen, the Recovery
Center's Phoenix leads the team back toward health, informed by
Scribe's records in the Journal Department.

The Command Center, with Echo and Chronos, is the nervous system
connecting all of this — messages and timing that let every department
act in concert. Reception, staffed by Herald, is where it's all first
seen.

## A Note on Canon

This lore describes *how it feels to watch the engine work* — it does
not change, replace, or contradict the actual responsibilities of the
trading agents. Where a department's story implies a responsibility,
that responsibility already exists in the engine; the World only
stages it.
